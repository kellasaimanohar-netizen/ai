# Right to Erasure Procedure

We support the right to erasure (Right to be Forgotten) for all users interacting with our agent.

- Users can type "Delete my data" or "Erase my information" to trigger an automated erasure workflow.
- This invokes the `erase_data` tool (when authorized), which scrubs conversation logs and removes personal identifiers from the cache.
- For complete account deletion, users are directed to the account settings page.
